1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Track Free (referred to as "we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a clean, offline-first personal habit tracker. We believe your personal habits, routines, self-improvement goals, and daily records are strictly confidential and belong solely to you.

The Fundamental Contract: We do not operate user account servers, we do not require your email address or phone number to use the app, and we do not monitor, harvest, or monetize your activity. The app is fully functional with airplane mode enabled.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All information you author or configure within Track Free is stored exclusively on your physical device in the application's isolated sandboxed storage (using SQLite and private Preferences). This data includes:

  • Habit Definitions: Title, description, schedule/frequency (daily, specific weekdays, or target times per week), routine categories, custom accent colors, icon glyphs, and emojis.
  • Check-in Logs & History: Daily completion status timestamps, streak counts, and modification audit timestamps.
  • User Preferences: Active color palettes, custom typography selections, dark/light theme configurations, and notification preferences.
  • Optional Display Name: An optional name or nickname you choose to enter for in-app dashboard greetings (stored locally only).
  • Anonymous Installation Identifier: A randomly generated UUID created upon installation. This identifier is used exclusively by the local sync merge engine to differentiate devices during two-way sync, preventing merge collisions. It is never linked to your personal identity.
  • Automated Rolling Backups: Automated local backup snapshots created within the app sandbox to protect you from accidental loss.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

To ensure complete clarity under global data privacy regulations:

  • We do not collect or store your real name, address, or payment credentials.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (e.g., IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android).
  • We do not capture device telemetry, GPS location, IP addresses, or network activity logs.
  • We do not read your contacts, photo library, microphone, camera, or files outside the app’s own sandbox.

4. Optional Cloud Synchronization

Track Free includes an optional, user-initiated cloud backup and synchronization feature. If you explicitly choose to enable sync, data is transferred directly between your device and your personal cloud storage provider over secure HTTPS/TLS encryption. No developer server ever touches or sees your data.

A. Android: Google Drive App Data Folder

When you enable Google Drive sync on Android:

  • The app requests the restricted Google Drive scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata.
  • Sandbox Isolation: This restricted scope grants Track Free access strictly to its own hidden, sandboxed Application Data folder. The app cannot view, access, modify, or delete any of your other documents, photos, or files in Google Drive.
  • Direct Connection: Your habit sync file (habit_tracker_sync_v1.json) is transmitted directly from your phone to Google's official Drive API endpoints over TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Google Account Information: During sign-in, Google displays your account email address purely within the on-device user interface so you know which Drive account is actively connected. We do not transmit or store this email on any external server.

B. iOS / macOS: Apple iCloud Documents

When you enable iCloud sync on Apple devices:

  • The app utilizes Apple's native iCloud Documents container (iCloud.co.trackfree.solforge).
  • Your sync payload is managed directly by Apple's operating system CloudKit daemon and synchronized across your authenticated Apple ID devices according to Apple's iCloud security standards.

6. Reminders & Local Notifications

All habit reminders are computed and scheduled locally on your physical device using the operating system's native notification system.

  • No push notification servers, third-party relay services (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging or OneSignal), or external webhooks are involved.
  • Reminder messages and habit titles are never sent over the Internet.
  • You can disable reminders globally in the app's Settings or via your operating system's Notification Settings at any time.

7. Home Screen Widgets

Track Free provides home screen widgets so you can view your today's habits and completion progress at a glance without opening the app:

  • Widget rendering utilizes an isolated, local shared application storage cache (App Group on iOS, SharedPreferences / app storage on Android).
  • The widget state contains only the summary required to display the widget visual (habit title, status, streak). It never communicates with external networks.

9. Data Deletion, Portability & Retention

Because all your data is stored locally on your device (or in your personal cloud container), you maintain unconditional control over its retention and deletion:

How to Delete Your Data Completely

  1. On-Device Deletion: Inside the app, navigate to Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete all data. This instantly and permanently drops all local SQLite tables, habits, check-ins, custom themes, and preferences.
  2. Uninstalling the App: Deleting or uninstalling the Track Free application from your device immediately purges all sandboxed app storage and local backups.
  3. Deleting Cloud Backups:
    • Google Drive: Open Google Drive in your web browser → Settings (gear icon) → Manage Apps → Track Free → Options → Delete hidden app data.
    • Apple iCloud: On your iOS device, open Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Track Free → Delete Data.

Data Portability / Export

You have the right to export your complete data at any time. In the app, navigate to Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data to download your entire habit history, check-in log, and themes in a clean, human-readable JSON format.

11. GDPR & UK Privacy Rights (EEA & UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK Data Protection Act 2018 provide specific statutory rights:

  • Data Controller Status: Because Track Free stores all data locally on your client device and transmits backups only to your personal authenticated cloud account, you are the sole custodian and controller of your habit data. The developer does not act as a processor or controller of your personal content.
  • Legal Basis for Processing: Any on-device processing is executed strictly pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR (performance of a contract to provide the habit tracking features you request).
  • Your Statutory Rights: You have the unconditional right to access, rectify, export (portability), or erase your personal data at any second directly via the in-app controls.
