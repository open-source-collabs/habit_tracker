## Summary
Track Free is a habit tracker that keeps your data on your device. There is no
Track Free account, no server, no advertising and no analytics. We (the
developer) never receive your habits or any personal data.

## Data stored on your device
Your habits, check-in history, reminder times, themes, the optional display
name you enter, notification preferences and a random installation ID (used
only to tell your own devices apart during sync) are stored locally in the
app's private storage. Automatic backups of this data are also kept on the
device.

## Optional cloud sync
If you turn on sync, a copy of the same data is saved to your own cloud
storage: the hidden app-data folder of your Google Drive (Android) or your
private iCloud container (Apple devices). For Google Drive, the app asks only
for access to its own app folder — it cannot see your other files — and shows
the email address of the signed-in account so you know which account is used.
This data is transferred directly between your device and Google or Apple over
an encrypted connection and is governed by their privacy policies. It is never
sent to us.

## Reminders
Reminders are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. No
notification service or push server is involved.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** — to show the reminders you set.
- **Run at startup** — to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
- **Internet** — used only for optional cloud sync.

## Sharing
We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. The app contains no
third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Deleting your data
**Settings → Delete all data** removes everything from this device;
uninstalling the app does too. To remove the cloud copy, turn off sync and
delete Track Free's data in Google Drive (**Settings → Manage apps**) or in
your iCloud storage settings.

## Children
Track Free is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address
and in the app, with a new effective date.
